How do friend requests work? ▼
Tap on the Friends tab in the bottom bar to review incoming requests. Tap "Confirm" to become mutual friends or "Delete" to decline.
How do I unlock NetPara Gold / VIP? ▼
Tap on "NetPara Gold" in the shortcuts menu to activate VIP verification and ad-free experience instantly for free.
How do I upload a Reel or Post? ▼
Tap the (+) button on the top right bar or the "What's happening?" card on the Home Feed. You can write text, attach images or video URLs.
